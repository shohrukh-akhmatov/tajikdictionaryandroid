Privacy Policy — Tajik Dictionary

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Tajik Dictionary app for Android and iOS (package tj.tajikdictionary), developed by Shohrukh Akhmatov ("we").

Summary

The dictionary and learning features work fully offline, and your saved words, history and progress stay on your device. We do not collect your personal data on our own servers. The app is free and shows ads served by Yandex Mobile Ads and Google AdMob, which collect some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

To provide its features, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Words you save to favorites and your search history
  • Flashcard, game and placement-test progress and streaks
  • Your settings (interface language, theme, font size, speech speed)

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to move it (see below). You can clear history and saved words in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Actions you choose to take

  • Export / import favorites: creates or reads a JSON file in a location you pick. We do not receive it.
  • Share: sends text or an image to the app you choose via the system share sheet.
  • Contact support: opens your email app; we only receive what you choose to send.
  • Pronunciation: uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine, governed by that engine's provider.
  • Device backup: your operating system may include app data in your personal device backup (e.g. Google account backup), under your account's settings.

Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads and Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after a finished word game. Each ad is requested from Yandex Mobile Ads (provided by YANDEX LLC) first and, if no Yandex ad is available, from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob, provided by Google LLC). Settings and the onboarding screens show no ads. When ads are shown, these SDKs may collect and share with their provider:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Interactions with ads and the app (for example ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostics, crash and performance data about the ads SDK

Yandex and Google use this data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where you have consented, to personalize ads. The app does not let the ad SDKs use your device's location (only an approximate location derived from your IP address may be used). Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. See the Yandex Privacy Policy, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK terms, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

The app contains no other analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes
